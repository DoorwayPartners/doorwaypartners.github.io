Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026
Last updated: November 8, 2026

Doorway Partners LLC (“Doorway Partners,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you visit doorwaypartners.com (the “Site”), contact us, or work with us in connection with our door-to-door fiber internet sales and marketing services.

This policy describes Doorway Partners LLC only. We are an independent sales and marketing company. We are not AT&T, T-Mobile, Fidium Fiber, Brightspeed, Frontier, or any other internet service provider. If you buy service through one of those providers, that provider’s own privacy policy also applies to the information it holds about your account.

Our services are intended for adults 18 years of age or older.

Information we collect

The Site is primarily informational. We do not currently require an account or use an online checkout form. We may collect:

  • Information you give us. If you email us, use the contact form on the Site, or otherwise contact us, we receive the information you include, such as your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, mailing address, the reason for your inquiry, and the content of your message.
  • Information from in-person or field conversations. If you speak with one of our representatives at your door or in your neighborhood, we may collect information you choose to share so we can follow up or help you start service with a fiber provider. That can include your name, address, phone number, email address, notes about your interest or appointment, and order details needed to submit a request to the provider.
  • Information from job applicants. If you ask about or apply for a role with us, we receive the information you send, as described in “Job applicants” below.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically collect standard log data when you visit the Site, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and the dates and times of your visits.

We do not currently use advertising cookies, third-party marketing pixels, or a separate analytics dashboard on the Site. If that changes, we will update this policy.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to questions and requests
  • Follow up after a conversation, appointment, or card exchange
  • Help you learn about and, if you choose, start fiber internet service with a provider we represent
  • Submit or support an order with the relevant provider when you ask us to
  • Review and respond to concerns, complaints, and requests not to be visited
  • Consider applicants for roles with us
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site
  • Send service or follow-up messages you have agreed to receive, including text messages where permitted
  • Meet legal, security, and recordkeeping obligations

Text messages

If you provide a mobile number and agree to hear from us by text, Doorway Partners LLC may send service, appointment, or follow-up messages related to your inquiry or order. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or email us at office@doorwaypartners.com. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. We do not sell your phone number to third-party marketers. Consent to text messages is not required as a condition of purchasing service from a fiber provider.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. That exclusion applies even where this policy describes other sharing of personal information.

Consent records. If you agree to receive texts from us, we keep a record of that consent so we can honor it and show that it was given, such as the phone number, the date and time, how consent was given, and the wording you agreed to. We also keep a record of opt-out requests so that we stop texting you. These records are used only for those purposes and are not shared with third parties. Giving us your phone number on the Site’s contact form does not, by itself, sign you up for text messages.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With the fiber provider you are interested in or sign up with, such as AT&T Fiber, T-Mobile Fiber, Fidium Fiber, Brightspeed, Frontier Fiber, and others, so they can process an inquiry, order, installation, or customer-support request
  • With authorized channel partners or program partners who work with us on a provider’s sales program, when that is needed to support a lead, order, or follow-up you asked us to handle
  • With service providers who help us operate our business, such as website hosting, email, CRM, digital business-card, messaging, and form-processing platforms, and who are only permitted to use the information to perform services for us
  • With FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), when you use the contact form or the rep application form on the Site. FormSubmit receives the information you enter so it can deliver your message to us at office@doorwaypartners.com, and is only permitted to use that information to process and deliver the submission
  • When required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger or sale of assets
  • With your direction or consent

The above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Job applicants

If you ask about or apply for a role, we use the information you send (such as your name, contact details, general location, and what you tell us about yourself) only to consider you for a role, communicate with you about it, and meet recordkeeping requirements. Applications go to Doorway Partners LLC.

Field sales roles with Doorway Partners are 1099 independent contractor positions, not employment.

Please do not send Social Security numbers, bank details, or copies of identity documents with an application. If you are hired, we will collect the information needed for onboarding through a separate process.

We keep applicant information only as long as needed to consider the role and meet legal or recordkeeping requirements. You can ask us to delete it by emailing office@doorwaypartners.com.

How we retain information

We keep information only as long as we have a reason to use it for the purposes described above. Inquiry and follow-up records are generally kept for up to two years, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or we need to keep them longer to support an order, handle a question or dispute, or meet a legal or recordkeeping requirement. Information related to an order we help submit may be kept as long as needed to support that request and to meet those same obligations. We delete or de-identify information when we no longer have a reason to keep it.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information, but no website, method of transmission, or method of storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, bank or card details, or passwords through the Site or by email.

Your choices and rights

You may email us to ask what personal information we have about you, to request a correction, or to ask us to delete information we are not required to keep. You may opt out of marketing texts as described above. If you have an account with a fiber provider, you will also need to contact that provider to manage information in its systems.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law. We will consider and respond to requests in accordance with those laws.

How to make a request

  • How to ask. Email office@doorwaypartners.com with the subject line “Privacy request.” Tell us what you are asking for (for example, access, correction, or deletion) and include the name, email address, phone number, or street address we may have on file for you.
  • How we confirm it’s you. To protect your information, we may ask you to confirm details we already have, or to reply from the email address or phone number we have on file, before we act on a request. We use the information you give us for verification only to handle your request.
  • Authorized agents. Someone you authorize may make a request for you. We may ask for written permission signed by you, and we may still need to confirm your identity directly.
  • Timing. We aim to respond within a reasonable time and within any period required by applicable law. If we need more time, or cannot fulfill a request (for example, because we must keep information to support an order or meet a legal obligation), we will tell you why.
  • No discrimination. We will not treat you differently or deny you anything because you made a privacy request.

Do Not Track and similar signals

Some browsers send “Do Not Track” or similar privacy signals. The Site does not currently use advertising cookies, third-party marketing pixels, or analytics tools that track you across websites, so there is no cross-site tracking for these signals to turn off. If we add such tools in the future, we will update this policy and explain how we respond to these signals.

Children

The Site and our services are intended for adults 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Links to other sites

The Site may mention or link to third-party providers or resources, including the official websites and availability checkers of AT&T Fiber, T-Mobile Fiber, Fidium Fiber, Brightspeed, Frontier Fiber, and others. When you follow those links, you leave the Site. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties. Their sites and services are governed by their own policies, which we encourage you to read.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. The updated policy will apply to information we collect after it is posted, and to existing information as described in the updated policy where permitted by law.
