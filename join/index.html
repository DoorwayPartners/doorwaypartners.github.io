Field sales with Doorway Partners

We’re looking for people who are comfortable talking with neighbors, want to learn, and care about representing fiber providers honestly. All agents and representatives are 1099 independent contractors, not employees.

Apply in about 2 minutesAsk a question

What the work involves

  • Door-to-door conversations in neighborhoods where AT&T Fiber, T-Mobile Fiber, Fidium Fiber, Brightspeed, Frontier Fiber and many more are available
  • Explaining options clearly and checking availability through the provider’s process
  • Following up with households that ask for it
  • Following provider guidelines, local rules and our code of conduct
  • Spending much of the day outdoors and on your feet

Who this is not for

  • Anyone looking for a desk or inside-sales role
  • Anyone uncomfortable hearing “no” often
  • Anyone who wants to win sales with pressure or exaggeration
  • Anyone unwilling to follow our code of conduct, provider rules and local solicitation rules

Mentorship, campaigns, and growth

Hands-on field mentorship

You’ll get hands-on field mentorship at the door, with guidance from the team.

Multi-carrier campaign access

Access campaigns across AT&T Fiber, T-Mobile Fiber, Fidium Fiber, Brightspeed, Frontier Fiber and many more.

Growth opportunities

As you gain experience, there is room to grow with the team.

Compensation

All Doorway Partners agents and representatives are 1099 independent contractors, not employees. Compensation depends on the role and the campaign. We’ll explain how pay works for a specific role, in writing, before you start. Individual results vary, and nothing on this website is a promise or estimate of earnings.

Applying or contacting us is not an offer of work. Any working relationship is set out in a separate written agreement.
