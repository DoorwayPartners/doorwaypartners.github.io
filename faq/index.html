Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about who we are and about joining the team.

About us and our work

Are you AT&T, T-Mobile, Fidium, Brightspeed or Frontier?

No. Doorway Partners LLC is an independent sales and marketing company. We market fiber internet from AT&T Fiber, T-Mobile Fiber, Fidium Fiber, Brightspeed, Frontier Fiber and many more, but we are not the internet provider.

Who bills me if I sign up?

The provider. Your service agreement, pricing, billing, equipment, installation and support are between you and the provider you choose. Doorway Partners does not bill you for internet service.

Do I have to buy anything if I talk with a rep?

No. Talking with a representative, taking a card or asking for a follow-up never obligates you to buy service.

Is fiber available at my address?

It depends on the provider and your exact address. Our reps can check, or you can check yourself on each provider’s official site: AT&T, T-Mobile Fiber, Fidium, Brightspeed, Frontier Fiber and others.

Who sets prices and promotions?

The provider. Plans, prices, availability and promotions can change, and the provider’s order process and disclosures control the final details.

How do I stop visits?

Tell the representative, or email office@doorwaypartners.com with your street address and ask not to be visited again. We’ll share it with our field team.

How do I check that a rep is with Doorway Partners?

Ask for the rep’s full name and email office@doorwaypartners.com with the name, your street and the date. We’ll confirm.

How do I report a concern?

Email office@doorwaypartners.com with what happened, when and where, and the rep’s name if you have it. We review every complaint and follow up by email.

Who do I contact about my internet bill, installation or an outage?

Your provider. They manage your account and service.

Will you text me?

Only if you give us your mobile number and agree to texts. You can reply STOP at any time. Our Privacy Policy explains our text-message practices.

For people interested in joining

How are reps paid?

All Doorway Partners agents and representatives are 1099 independent contractors, not employees. Compensation depends on the role and the campaign. We explain how pay works for a specific role, in writing, before anyone starts. Individual results vary, and nothing on this website is a promise of earnings.

Do I need sales experience?

Not necessarily. What matters most is being comfortable talking with people, willing to learn, and committed to being honest at the door.

Is this door-to-door work?

Yes. It’s in-person, residential field sales, with much of the day spent outdoors and on your feet.

Does applying mean I have a job?

No. Applying or contacting us is not an offer of work. All Doorway Partners agents and representatives are 1099 independent contractors, not employees. Any working relationship is set out in a separate written agreement.

What should I not send when applying?

Please don’t send your Social Security number, bank details or copies of ID documents. If you’re hired, we’ll collect what’s needed securely during onboarding.

How do I apply?

Use the contact form and choose “Join the team,” or email office@doorwaypartners.com.
