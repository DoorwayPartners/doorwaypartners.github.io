How we work

From training to an installed customer

We represent fiber providers in neighborhoods where their service is available. The provider handles installation, billing and support.

  1. Train on the offer

    Representatives learn the provider’s product, ordering process and guidelines before anyone knocks.

  2. Work the right neighborhoods

    We focus on areas where the provider’s service is available and follow local solicitation rules.

  3. Honest conversations, then the provider takes over

    Reps explain options accurately and respect a “no.” If someone chooses to sign up, the provider installs and bills them.
