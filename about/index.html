About Doorway Partners

An independent door-to-door fiber sales and marketing company based in Falcon, Colorado.

Who we are

Doorway Partners LLC (“Doorway Partners”) is an independently owned and operated sales and marketing company. We help households learn about fiber internet from AT&T Fiber, T-Mobile Fiber, Fidium Fiber, Brightspeed, Frontier Fiber, Metronet, Mountain High and many more, and help them start service with the provider if they choose to.

We are not an internet service provider. When a household signs up, the provider handles the service, installation, billing and customer support.

“Doorway Partners” is the name we use day to day. Our legal name is Doorway Partners LLC.

How we work: our field code of conduct

Say who we are

Our reps identify themselves as Doorway Partners and make clear we are independent, not the provider.

Be accurate

We explain only what the provider actually offers at an address, and point people to the provider for final details.

Respect the answer

“No,” “not now” and “please don’t come back” are all respected.

Follow the rules

We work to follow provider guidelines and local solicitation rules where we work.

Protect information

We collect only what’s needed to follow up or help with an order. See our Privacy Policy.

Own our mistakes

If something goes wrong, we want to hear about it and make it right where we can.

Concerns and complaints

Email office@doorwaypartners.com with what happened, when and where. We review every complaint and follow up by email. You can also ask us not to visit your home again.
