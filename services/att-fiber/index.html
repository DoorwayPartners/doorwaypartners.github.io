Services

Door-to-door sales and marketing for AT&T Fiber

We talk with households in neighborhoods where AT&T Fiber is offered, explain the options, and help people start service with AT&T if they choose to.

What we do

  • Introduce AT&T Fiber in neighborhoods where it’s offered
  • Check what AT&T shows as available at your address
  • Explain the options in plain language and answer questions
  • Help you start an order through AT&T’s process, if you decide to
  • Follow up if you ask us to

What AT&T does

  • Sets plans, prices, availability and promotions
  • Processes and approves your order
  • Installs your service
  • Bills you and provides customer support
